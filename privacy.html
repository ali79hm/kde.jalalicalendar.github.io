Privacy policy

Your calendar stays yours.

Jalali Calendar is designed to work locally on your KDE Plasma desktop.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

At a glance

Jalali Calendar does not collect personal information on developer-operated servers, sell user data, or use user data for advertising.

The widget runs on your device. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Jalali Calendar accesses the Google Calendar data permitted by the OAuth permissions you approve only to provide Google Calendar functionality inside the widget.

Information sent to the developer

Jalali Calendar does not require a Jalali Calendar account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry.

Your Google Calendar events, Google account data, and calendar information are not sent to the developer or stored on a developer-operated server.

Google user data accessed

Connecting a Google Account is optional. If you enable Google Calendar integration, Jalali Calendar requests access only to the Google Calendar information allowed by the OAuth permissions that you approve on Google's consent screen.

The Google user data accessed by Jalali Calendar may include your calendar list and calendar information, as well as calendar event information such as event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, and other event details made available through the authorized Google Calendar permissions.

Jalali Calendar does not access Google user data outside the permissions granted by you.

How Google user data is used

Jalali Calendar uses Google user data only to provide Google Calendar functionality requested by the user.

The application retrieves the calendars and events you authorize so that they can be displayed in the Jalali Calendar widget on your KDE Plasma desktop.

Google user data is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics, artificial intelligence training, machine-learning training, or any other unrelated purpose.

Jalali Calendar does not sell Google user data.

Protection of Google user data

Google Calendar information is processed locally on your device. Jalali Calendar does not maintain a developer-operated database containing your Google Calendar events or other Google user data.

Communication between Jalali Calendar and Google services uses secure encrypted HTTPS connections provided by Google's APIs and authentication services.

Access to Google user data is limited to the permissions explicitly approved by you through Google's OAuth authorization process.

Authorization credentials required to maintain the Google Calendar connection may be stored locally on your device or by your desktop account or authentication service. They are not intentionally sent to or stored on developer-operated servers.

Sharing of Google user data

Jalali Calendar does not share Google user data with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or unrelated third parties.

Google user data is exchanged only between your device and Google's services as necessary to provide the Google Calendar functionality you requested.

Google user data retention and deletion

Jalali Calendar does not retain an independent server-side copy of your Google Calendar data.

Calendar information retrieved from Google is processed locally as needed to provide calendar functionality.

You can stop Jalali Calendar from accessing your Google data at any time by disconnecting your Google Account from the application or by revoking Jalali Calendar's access from your Google Account permissions.

After access is revoked, Jalali Calendar can no longer retrieve new Google Calendar data.

Locally stored authorization credentials or cached Google Calendar information can be removed by disconnecting the Google integration and removing Jalali Calendar's locally stored application data.

Device access

Jalali Calendar may read the current system date, time, locale, appearance settings, and your selected calendar preferences so it can display the correct calendar and match your KDE Plasma desktop.

This information remains on your device.

This website

This website is a static website. It does not use a Jalali Calendar account system, advertising, tracking pixels, first-party analytics, or contact forms.

The hosting provider may process basic technical information required to deliver the website according to its own privacy policy.

External services

Jalali Calendar connects to Google services only when you enable Google Calendar integration.

The website may also contain links to services such as KDE Store and GitHub. Your use of those external services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to this policy

If Jalali Calendar or this website changes in a way that affects how user data is accessed, used, stored, shared, protected, retained, or deleted, this privacy policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

Questions

For privacy questions or project issues, please use the project's GitHub page.
