Privacy policy

Your calendar stays yours.

Jalali Calendar is designed to work locally on your KDE Plasma desktop.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

At a glance

Jalali Calendar does not collect personal information on developer-operated servers, sell user data, or use data for advertising. The widget runs on your device. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, it accesses the calendar data you authorize only to display your events in the widget.

Information sent to the developer

The widget does not require a Jalali Calendar account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry. Your calendar events and Google account data are not sent to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

Google Calendar access

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you enable it, Google asks you to authorize the permissions needed by the widget. The widget uses that access only to retrieve and display the calendars and events you select. It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage and sharing of Google data

The widget does not save independent copies of your Google Calendar events or share them with the developer or other third parties. Event data is processed locally to provide the calendar display. Google or your desktop account service may retain authorization credentials needed to keep your account connected. You can revoke the widget’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Device access

The widget may read the current system date, time, locale, appearance settings, and your selected calendar preferences so it can show the correct calendar and match your Plasma desktop. This information remains on your device.

This website

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. The site itself does not use cookies, account systems, contact forms, or first-party analytics. GitHub may process basic technical information when serving the site under its own privacy terms.

External links

The widget may connect to Google services when you enable Google Calendar. The download page also links to KDE Store and GitHub. Your use of those services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Changes to this policy

If the widget or website changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above will be revised.

Questions

For privacy questions or project issues, please use the project’s GitHub page.
